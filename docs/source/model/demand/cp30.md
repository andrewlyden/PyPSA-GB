# CP30 2030 (brownfield)

Scenarios built on NESO Clean Power 2030 (CP30) with `method: brownfield` on the ETYS network build demand from parts,
in `scripts/demand/cp30_demand.py`. CP30's workbook gives national totals: ED1 (demand by sector, GWh) and ES1 (the
pathway's dispatch-model outputs). Reduced and Zonal CP30 builds keep the FES GSP-block method ({doc}`fes_gsp_blocks`).

## Method and assumptions

1. **Energy.** The consumer total is CP30 ED1 (residential, industrial and commercial, road transport). ED1's system
   total, which includes losses, sets one loss factor (system / consumer) applied pro rata to every load.
2. **Named transmission loads.** Data centres, rail and HS2 feeders, St Fergus and the industrial direct connections
   from the FES 2024 ETYS regional breakdown sit at their own ETYS nodes. Their energy is FES 2024 ED1's
   transmission data-centre and direct-customer lines, split by site peak. Rule `extract_cp30_demand_inputs` writes
   the table; each hand-chosen node is evidenced in `data/demand/cp30_named_load_nodes.csv`.
3. **GSP residual.** Only what is left is spread on FES 2024 Holistic Transition Dem_BB003 GSP shares (the FES
   edition CP30 was built on). FES's `Direct(SPTL)` and `Direct(SHETL)` rows are spread within their own
   transmission owner's area.
4. **Components inside each GSP.** EVs, heat pumps, district heat and distribution-connected data centres take their
   ED1 energy at their own FES GSP shares (Dem_BB004 to Dem_BB008). The ESPENI base takes the rest of each GSP's energy.
5. **Shapes.** Heat pumps and district heat follow the weather year's demand-weighted temperature (heating degree
   hours and a temperature-dependent coefficient of performance). EVs follow plug-in charging profiles, with a smart
   share moved to an overnight window and solved so EV load at peak equals FES's "EVs at peak". Data centres and named
   loads are flat.
6. **Peak calibration.** The base shape $b_t$ is raised to a power $\alpha$ and rescaled to keep its energy $E_b$:

   $$
   d_t = E_b \, \frac{b_t^{\alpha}}{\sum_s b_s^{\alpha}\,\Delta t} + c_t ,
   $$

   where $c_t$ is the sum of the component loads. $\alpha$ is found by bisection within `shape.exponent_bounds` so
   that $\max_t d_t$ equals `shape.peak_target`: `es1` (CP30 ES1 demand excluding electrolysis, CP30's own dispatch
   peak), `ed1` (the FES average cold spell peak), or a number in MW. Energy is exact whatever $\alpha$ is.

The GSP residual and components are placed on the network with the `same_site` bus capacity rule ({doc}`placement`).
Consumer flexibility is carved from these components ({doc}`../flexibility/consumer`).

## Data

| Input | File | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| CP30 workbook tables | `resources/CP30/CP30_ED1.csv`, `CP30_ES1.csv` | CP30 totals and peak |
| FES 2024 workbooks | `data/FES/`, `data/network/ETYS/Regional breakdown of FES24 data.xlsx` | named loads, GSP and component shares |
| Named-load nodes | `data/demand/cp30_named_load_nodes.csv` | node choice per named load, with evidence |
| ESPENI | `data/demand/espeni.csv` | base shape |
| Weather cutout | `resources/atlite/cutouts/uk-{year}.nc` | temperature for heat shapes |

## Configuration

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `demand.build.enabled` | `true` under `method/brownfield.yaml` | CP30 component-built demand (ETYS only) |
| `demand.build.spatial` | FES 2024 Holistic Transition (CP30) | FES edition of the GSP shares |
| `demand.build.named_loads` | on | named transmission loads at their nodes |
| `demand.build.shape.peak_target` | `es1` (CP30) | `es1`, `ed1` or MW |
| `demand.build.shape.exponent_bounds` | `[0.2, 4.0]` | range of $\alpha$ |

The brownfield build's settings are in `config/defaults/method/brownfield.yaml` and the CP30 inputs in
`config/defaults/source/CP30.yaml`; `scripts/demand/cp30_demand.py`'s `DEFAULT_CONFIG` must equal the block.

## Validation

- CP30 builds compare network demand with CP30 ES1 demand excluding electrolysis
  (`resources/CP30/validation/{scenario}_energy_totals.csv`, rule `validate_CP30_network`). After Stage 1, demand
  more than 0.5% from ES1 fails the Stage 1 check (rule `validate_CP30_stage1`).
- The CP30 locational checks (rule `validate_CP30_locational`) test each node's peak load plus electrolysis against
  what its circuits can import at the ratings the balancing stage enforces, and the named loads against their
  references.

## Limitations

- The ESPENI base is a historical year's shape (CP30's own weather year, 2013), reshaped only by the peak exponent.
- The named loads are flat.
